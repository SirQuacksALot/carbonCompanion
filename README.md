### Hi! I'm your Carbon Companion!

Tap me and I'll page you through the **net carbon balance** of an AI that manages energy — the CO₂e it saves minus the CO₂e it costs itself (hardware, data transfer, training, inference).

Across six topics — net balance, footprint, uncertainty, break-even, system boundary and impact — I react emotionally: my face and colour change with the result, and a permanently visible gauge shows whether the AI is worth it. Every parameter is adjustable in the **debug menu** (⚙, top right).

I speak six languages: English · Deutsch · Polski · Italiano · Español · Français.
